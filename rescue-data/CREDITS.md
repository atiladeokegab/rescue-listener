# Credits

## Knock recordings (knocks/, and the knocks inside sequences/ and demo/)

"Knocking Sound Effects With Emotional Intentions" by Adrián Barahona-Ríos and Sandra Pauletto,
performed by foley artist Ulf Olausson at FoleyWorks, Stockholm (2019). Zenodo, 2020.
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.3668503 — https://zenodo.org/records/3668503
Credit: Barahona-Ríos & Pauletto 2020, CC BY 4.0.
Licensed under CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made: 20 single knocks cut from the first knock of 20 recorded actions, trimmed, faded,
resampled from 48 kHz to 16 kHz mono and peak-normalised; then placed at new times and mixed with
synthetic noise in sequences/ and demo/. The source files used are listed in sequences.json (knock_pool).

## Everything else

Debris impacts, settling grit, drone rotor hum and distant machinery are synthetic, generated by scripts/build.py from
fixed seeds. No third-party audio is used for them.
