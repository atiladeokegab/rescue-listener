Idea from Eytan, Brenner & Marom 2003: in rat cortical networks, responses to an input that kept repeating (our background rumble) faded while responses to an occasional one (our knock) held up.
This is a faster, shorter, gentler version (2 Hz, 3 min, 2 µA), not the paper's protocol. The index is Ulanovsky's (2003). Exploratory.
